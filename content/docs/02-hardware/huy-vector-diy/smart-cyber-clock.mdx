## Required Libraries (Arduino / PlatformIO)

- `Adafruit_GFX`
- `Adafruit_ST7735` and `Adafruit_ST7789`
- `Adafruit_AHTX0`
- `ENS160` — ScioSense ENS160 Library (Adafruit Fork)
